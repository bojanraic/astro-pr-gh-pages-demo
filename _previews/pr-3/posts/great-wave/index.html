The Great Wave off Kanagawa

Katsushika Hokusai's woodblock print is the most recognizable work to come out of the ukiyo-e movement. It captures a moment of scale: three fishing boats dwarfed by a cresting wave, with Mount Fuji rendered small in the background.

The composition's sense of vastness echoes a very different subject: seeEarthrise, a photograph taken from lunar orbit that gave humanity its first view of Earth as a small, fragile whole.

For a landscape captured on Earth rather than above it, seeGrand Prismatic Spring.
