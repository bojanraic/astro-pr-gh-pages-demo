Grand Prismatic Spring

The largest hot spring in the United States, Grand Prismatic Spring owes its concentric bands of color to heat-loving microorganisms living at different temperatures across the pool. From the air, the pattern resembles an eye.

Seen from far enough away, terrestrial features start to look like the view from orbit — see Earthrise for the inverse: Earth itself seen small and whole.

And for a very different kind of natural spectacle rendered by human hands, see The Great Wave off Kanagawa.
