Earthrise

Taken by astronaut William Anders as Apollo 8 emerged from behind the Moon, this photograph is often credited with shifting public perception of Earth as a single, bounded system rather than an endless backdrop.

That shift in scale rhymes with an aerial view taken much closer to home:Grand Prismatic Spring, where distance turns a hot spring into an abstract pattern of color.

For a wave-scale view of nature's power from the ground, seeThe Great Wave off Kanagawa.
